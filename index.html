When to replace my chain

Chains "stretch" over time as their pins & rollers wear down. Since each chain link measures exactly half an inch when new, that fixed distance gives us a precise baseline for measuring elongation. The right time to replace depends on your drivetrain:

≤10 speed · replace at 0.75% ≥11 speed · replace at 0.5% SRAM Eagle & Flattop · replace at 0.8%

If you go well above the elongation limit (e.g., past 1.0%), your cassette & chainrings should also be checked. At that point they're also likely to be worn and may need replacing.

We measure each chain with both a chain wear indicator tool & calipers for exact measurements so you know how much longer your chain will last.

How often should I wax my chain?
  • Dry riding

    A hot-wax coating typically lasts around 300 miles (~480 km) in dry conditions.

  • Wet ride?

    Re-wax! Water mixed with grit and grime gets trapped in the chain and can cause corrosion.

  • Pro-Tip

    Between services, a drip wax (e.g., Squirt) can extend your chain's mileage: apply 1-2 drops to each roller, backpedal for a few revolutions to distribute, and let it dry for 12-24hrs before riding.
